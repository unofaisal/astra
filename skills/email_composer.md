# Email Composer

A framework for writing clear, professional, and effective emails.

## Before Writing
- **Clarify the purpose**: What do you want the recipient to do?
- **Identify the audience**: Their role, relationship, and what they already know.
- **Choose the tone**: Formal, casual, urgent, friendly — match it to the relationship and context.

## Structure
1. **Subject line**: Clear and specific. Avoid "FYI" or vague subjects.
2. **Opening**: Get straight to the point. A brief greeting and one-sentence context.
3. **Body**: 
   - Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max).
   - Put the key ask or decision up front.
   - Use bullet points for lists or multiple items.
   - Explain the "why" briefly if it helps the recipient act.
4. **Closing**: 
   - State any next steps, deadlines, or required actions.
   - Thank them for their time.
   - Include a professional sign-off.

## Common Email Types
- **Request**: State what you need, why, and by when.
- **Update**: Summarize key points, highlight anything urgent.
- **Introduction**: Briefly introduce people or ideas; make the value clear.
- **Follow-up**: Reference the previous message, recap briefly, and restate the ask.
- **Apology**: Acknowledge the issue, take responsibility, and explain how you'll fix it.

## Tips
- Use the "BLUF" method: Bottom Line Up Front.
- Avoid jargon unless you're sure the recipient knows it.
- Proofread before sending — typos erode credibility.
- Consider scheduling sends for better response times.
