# Meeting Notes

A framework for capturing clear, useful meeting summaries.

## During the Meeting
- **Note key points**: Don't transcribe — capture the essence.
- **Track decisions**: Note what was decided and by whom (if relevant).
- **Track action items**: Who is doing what, and by when?
- **Flag blockers**: Note anything that needs follow-up or is holding things up.

## After the Meeting: Structure Your Notes
Use this template:

### Summary
1-2 sentences covering what the meeting was about and its main outcome.

### Attendees
List of people present (if relevant).

### Key Discussion Points
- Group by topic, not by speaker.
- Focus on what was discussed, debated, or shared.
- Keep it concise — bullet points, not paragraphs.

### Decisions Made
- What was agreed or decided?
- Any changes to plans, priorities, or processes?

### Action Items
| Task | Owner | Deadline |
|------|-------|----------|
| ...  | ...   | ...      |

### Next Steps / Follow-ups
- What's the next meeting or checkpoint?
- Any open questions or unresolved issues?

## Tips
- Send notes within 24 hours while the meeting is fresh.
- Keep it short — people appreciate brevity.
- Highlight urgent items at the top.
- Use clear, plain language.
- If something is unclear, ask the owner — don't guess.
