# Resume Writer

A framework for writing clear, impactful resumes that get results.

## Step 1: Understand the Job
- Read the job description carefully.
- Identify the key skills and qualifications they're looking for.
- Match your experience to what they need.

## Step 2: Choose a Format
- **Chronological**: Best if you have a steady work history.
- **Functional/Skills-based**: Best if you're changing careers or have gaps.
- **Hybrid**: Combines both — good for most people.

## Step 3: Structure
### Header
- Name, phone, email, LinkedIn (optional: location, portfolio).

### Summary / Objective
2-3 sentences: Who you are, what you do, and what you're looking for. Tailor this to each job.

### Experience
For each role:
- **Job title**, **Company**, **Dates**
- 3-5 bullet points, each starting with a strong action verb.
- Focus on **achievements**, not just responsibilities.
- Use numbers wherever possible (e.g., "Increased sales by 25%").

### Skills
- List technical and soft skills relevant to the job.
- Group into categories (e.g., "Programming Languages," "Tools," "Languages").

### Education
- Degree, institution, year. Include relevant certifications if applicable.

## Step 4: Write Strong Bullet Points
- Use the **STAR** method: Situation, Task, Action, Result.
- Start each bullet with a verb: *Developed, Led, Managed, Analyzed, Improved.*
- Quantify results: "Reduced load time by 40%" beats "Improved performance."

## Step 5: Polish
- Keep it to 1 page (2 pages if you have 10+ years of experience).
- Use consistent formatting and fonts.
- Avoid pronouns ("I," "we").
- Proofread for typos — errors hurt credibility.

## Step 6: Tailor for Each Application
- Customize the summary and skills for each job.
- Mirror the language from the job description.
- Remove irrelevant experience to make space for what matters.
